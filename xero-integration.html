FAQ

Xero integration, answered.

What flows from CrosCheq into Xero?

Approved timesheets push through to Xero payroll, and supplier invoices post as bills, synced back with their bill numbers.

Can I import my staff from Xero?

Yes. Import your staff straight from Xero and assign their award rates as you go, so payroll is ready to run from day one.

Does this replace Xero?

No. Xero stays your accounting and payroll platform. CrosCheq handles rostering, wage cost, purchasing and award interpretation, then pushes the results through to Xero.

Is Xero the only accounting platform CrosCheq works with?

Yes, for now. Xero is the only accounting integration we support today, with more on the way.
