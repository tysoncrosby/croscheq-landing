FAQ

Pricing, answered.

What does the price include?

Everything. There are no tiers, per-seat fees or paid add-ons. One flat monthly price per store covers rostering, timesheets, ordering, recipe costing, staff onboarding, CrosCheq Sign and the integrations.

How does the founding offer work?

The first 20 venues to join pay 50% off the standard price for as long as they stay, which is $149.50 per month instead of $299. Enter the code FOUNDING20 in the promo code box at checkout.

Will I be charged during the free trial?

No. You will be asked for a card when you start, but you will not be charged today. After the 30 days it is billed monthly.

Is there a contract?

No lock-in. It is billed monthly and you can cancel any time from Settings.

I run more than one venue. How does pricing work?

Pricing is per store, and each venue has its own login. If you run several, email support@croscheq.com and we will help you get set up.
