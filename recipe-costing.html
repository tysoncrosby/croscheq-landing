FAQ

Recipe costing, answered.

Where do the ingredient prices come from?

From the same supplier prices already flowing into CrosCheq through purchasing, so a recipe's cost reflects what you are actually paying, not a price entered once and forgotten.

What happens when a supplier price changes?

Cost per serve, sell price margin and gross profit recalculate automatically for every recipe using that ingredient.

Can I set a target margin and get warned when a dish drops under it?

Yes. Set a target gross profit per dish, and CrosCheq flags it the moment a recipe drops under target.

Does this replace my recipe cards?

No. It costs the recipes you already run, ingredients, quantities and yield per batch, so cost per serve stays accurate as prices move.
