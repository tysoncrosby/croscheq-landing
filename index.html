FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does CrosCheq replace Xero and Square, or work alongside them?

Alongside. CrosCheq pulls sales straight from your POS and syncs wages through to Xero. You keep the accounting and payments tools you already use. Rostering, wage cost, purchasing and award interpretation happen in CrosCheq.

How long does it take to get set up?

Most venues are rostering in CrosCheq within a day. Connect your POS and Xero, add your staff and their awards, and you are tracking live wage cost from the first roster you build.

What does it cost?

$299 a month. Founding venues get 50% off for as long as they stay, locking in $149.50 a month before general pricing goes live.

Is my venue's data safe?

Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted on secure cloud infrastructure, and kept separate from every other venue on CrosCheq. We do not sell or share your data.

Can I bring across my existing staff and rosters?

You can import your staff straight from Xero and assign their award rates as you go. Rosters themselves are not imported, but the day builder makes creating one fast, so you will have your first roster running in minutes.

How do I get my products and invoices into CrosCheq?

As easily as your suppliers issue them. Import supplier invoices straight into CrosCheq and AI pulls the line items to create your products automatically, ready to flow straight into live recipe costing.

Do I need an invite to sign up?

Yes, for now. CrosCheq is invite-only while we onboard the first wave of venues. Request access and we will be in touch.
